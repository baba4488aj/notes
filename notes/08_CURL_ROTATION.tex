Curl and Rotation

Vorticity is velocity CURL:
$$\vec\zeta=\nabla\times\vec V$$
Components:
$$\zeta_x=w_y-v_z$$
$$\zeta_y=u_z-w_x$$
$$\zeta_z=v_x-u_y$$
Angular rotation is HALF vorticity:
$$\vec\Omega=\frac{\vec\zeta}{2}$$
For 2D flow:
$$\Omega_z=\frac{1}{2}(v_x-u_y)$$
Positive Omega_z: CCW local spin.
Negative: clockwise.
Units: 1/s.

HOW TO TEST IRROTATIONAL
All components of curl must equal 0 THROUGHOUT the region. Zero at a point/line does not prove global irrotationality.

EXAMPLE
$u=2xy$, $v=-x^2y$:
$$\Omega_z=-x(y+1)$$
Zero at x=0 or y=-1, but rotational generally.

TRAPS
Incompressible is NOT the same as irrotational.
Curved streamlines do not necessarily indicate local rotation.
For straight shear $u=cy$, v=0:
$$\zeta_z=-c$$
