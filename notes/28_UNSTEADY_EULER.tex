Unsteady Bernoulli

INCOMPRESSIBLE INVISCID INSTANTANEOUS STREAMLINE, coordinate s from 1 to 2:
$$\int_1^2\frac{\partial V}{\partial t}ds$$
$$+\frac{V_2^2-V_1^2}{2}$$
$$+\frac{p_2-p_1}{\rho}+g(z_2-z_1)=0$$
Extra integral is LOCAL acceleration with units m^2/s^2.

UNIFORM STRAIGHT TUBE of length L, area constant and V=V(t):
$$\int_1^2\frac{\partial V}{\partial t}ds$$
$$=L\frac{dV}{dt}$$
Since same V at both ends, kinetic terms cancel:
$$L\frac{dV}{dt}=\frac{p_1-p_2}{\rho}$$
$$+g(z_1-z_2)$$

EXAMPLE
Water rho=1000; horizontal L=5m; pressure difference 10 kPa, no friction:
$$\frac{dV}{dt}=\frac{10000}{1000(5)}$$
= 2 m/s^2. For V(0)=0: V=2t under this ideal early-startup model.

VARIABLE CROSS-SECTION
For Q(t) and A(s):
$$V(s,t)=\frac{Q(t)}{A(s)}$$
Local inertia integral:
$$\frac{dQ}{dt}\int_1^2\frac{ds}{A(s)}$$
End kinetic terms generally differ.

Incompressible DOES NOT mean local acceleration equals zero.
