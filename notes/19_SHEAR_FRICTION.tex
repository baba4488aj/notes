Shear and Friction

PIPE WALL SHEAR FROM u(r)
$$\tau_{rz}=\mu\frac{du}{dr}$$
$$=\frac{r}{2}\frac{dp}{dz}$$
Using driving gradient $G=-dp/dz$:
$$\tau_{rz}=-\frac{Gr}{2}$$

MAGNITUDE AT WALL
$$|\tau_w|=\frac{GR}{2}$$
$$|\tau_w|=\frac{4\mu\bar V}{R}$$
$$|\tau_w|=\frac{8\mu\bar V}{D}$$
Stress zero on centerline, greatest magnitude at wall.

PRESSURE vs FORCE (horizontal pipe)
$$\Delta p\,\pi R^2$$
$$=|\tau_w|2\pi RL$$

FANNING COEFFICIENT
$$C_f=\frac{|\tau_w|}{\rho\bar V^2/2}$$
DARCY FRICTION FACTOR
$$f_D=4C_f$$

LAMINAR ROUND PIPE
$$C_f=\frac{16}{Re_D}$$
$$f_D=\frac{64}{Re_D}$$

PLATES with full gap h and very wide walls:
$$|\tau_w|=\frac{6\mu\bar V}{h}$$
$$C_f=\frac{12}{Re_h}$$
With $D_h\approx 2h$:
$$f_D=\frac{96}{Re_{Dh}}$$

Never confuse Darcy f with Fanning Cf. Moody chart uses Darcy f.
