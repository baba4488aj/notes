Plane Poiseuille

BOTH WALLS FIXED
$u(0)=u(h)=0$. Full gap = h.
$$u(y)=\frac{G}{2\mu}y(h-y)$$
$$u_{max}=\frac{Gh^2}{8\mu}$$
$$\bar V=\frac{Gh^2}{12\mu}$$
$$\bar V=\frac{2}{3}u_{max}$$
$$Q=bh\bar V$$

HORIZONTAL PRESSURE DROP
$$\Delta p=\frac{12\mu L\bar V}{h^2}$$
$$\Delta p=\frac{12\mu LQ}{bh^3}$$

CENTERED COORDINATE
Let $\eta=y-h/2$, so walls are at eta = +/-h/2:
$$u=\frac{G}{2\mu}(\frac{h^2}{4}-\eta^2)$$
Another convention with walls at +/-a has h=2a. Using a in place of full h changes factors by FOUR.

PLATE WALL SHEAR
$$|\tau_w|=\frac{Gh}{2}$$
$$|\tau_w|=\frac{6\mu\bar V}{h}$$

PLATE FRICTION (wide stationary gap)
Define $Re_h=\rho\bar Vh/\mu$:
$$C_f=\frac{12}{Re_h}$$
Hydraulic diameter Dh about 2h:
$$C_f=\frac{24}{Re_{Dh}}$$
$$f_D=\frac{96}{Re_{Dh}}$$
Do NOT substitute Dh into circular-pipe 64/Re for laminar plate flow.
