Common Exam Traps

STEADY does NOT imply zero convective acceleration.
INCOMPRESSIBLE does NOT imply irrotational.
FULLY DEVELOPED does NOT imply dp/dx=0.
ZERO SHEAR at free surface does NOT imply zero surface velocity.
ZERO NET FLOW Q=0 does NOT imply u(y)=0 everywhere.
NEGATIVE GAUGE p is not necessarily negative ABSOLUTE pressure.

GAUGE PRESSURE
Set p=0 only at atmospheric point AFTER choosing gauge reference.

ENERGY
Always use actual flow direction; loss HL >=0 on downstream side in the upstream+added = downstream+loss form.
Cancel velocity heads only if both speed and alphaE agree.
Rising pipe may lose p without dissipative loss.
Downhill pipe may gain static p while total head falls.
Contraction can reduce p ideally without loss.
Reservoir exit dissipates kinetic energy; free jet retains it.

VISCOUS
Use whole gap h vs half-gap a correctly.
Use $dA=2\pi r\,dr$ for pipe integration.
Use signed $\tau=\mu u'$ for stress component; wall force direction also requires face normal.
Darcy and Fanning differ by factor 4.
Do not reuse fixed K without matching geometry.

FIELD DIFFERENTIATION
Differentiate BEFORE substituting point.
For vector angle, check quadrant.
Euler gradient includes gravity ONLY on vertical axes.

COURSE-SPECIFIC
Laminar entrance length estimate: 0.06 Re D.
$H_L$ is meters; $h_L=gH_L$ is J/kg.
Course Couette alphaC and energy alphaE unrelated.
