Extended Energy

FOLLOW FLOW FROM STATION 1 TO 2.

COMPLETE HEAD BALANCE
$$\frac{p_1}{\rho g}+\frac{\alpha_{E1}\bar V_1^2}{2g}$$
$$+z_1+H_p$$
$$=\frac{p_2}{\rho g}+\frac{\alpha_{E2}\bar V_2^2}{2g}$$
$$+z_2+H_t+H_L$$

Hp = pump head ADDED to fluid.
Ht = turbine head REMOVED.
HL >= 0 = total irreversible loss along FLOW direction.

MEMORY RULE
Upstream energy + added = downstream energy + extracted + losses.

LECTURE NOTATION
For energy per mass (J/kg), multiply head equation by g; replace HL in m by hL=gHL in J/kg.

ENERGY CORRECTION
Use averaged V with:
$$\alpha_E=\frac{\int_Au^3dA}{A\bar V^3}$$
Uniform profile alphaE=1.
Laminar pipe alphaE=2.
Fixed-plate Poiseuille alphaE=54/35.
For high-Re pipe model alphaE about 1.

Use local speed in streamline Bernoulli. Do NOT multiply Darcy head loss by alphaE.

HEAD LOSS WITH NO MACHINES
$$H_L=\frac{p_1-p_2}{\rho g}$$
$$+\frac{\alpha_{E1}\bar V_1^2-\alpha_{E2}\bar V_2^2}{2g}$$
$$+z_1-z_2$$
If same z and same speed/alpha: simply pressure drop divided by rho*g.
