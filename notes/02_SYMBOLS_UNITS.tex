Symbols and Units

Velocity:
$u(y)$ or $u(r)$ = local axial velocity.
$\bar V$ = cross-sectional mean speed.
$U$ = specified wall speed (signed).
$Q$ = volume flow, m^3/s.
$\dot m=\rho Q$ = mass flow, kg/s.

LECTURE LOSS CONVENTION
$h_L$ = mechanical energy loss per mass (J/kg = m^2/s^2).
$H_L$ = head loss in meters.
$$h_L=gH_L$$
Some books instead use h_L for loss in meters. Check units.

The Couette parameter $\alpha_C$ is NOT the kinetic-energy correction $\alpha_E$.

FLUID PROPERTIES
$$\nu=\frac{\mu}{\rho}$$
$$\gamma=\rho g$$
$$SG=\frac{\rho}{\rho_{water}}$$
$\mu$ = Pa s; $\nu$ = m^2/s; $\rho$ = kg/m^3.

GEOMETRY AND UNITS
$$A=\frac{\pi D^2}{4}=\pi R^2$$
1 L/min = 0.001/60 m^3/s.
1 in = 0.0254 m; 1 ft = 0.3048 m.
1 kPa = 1000 Pa.

PRESSURE
$$p_{abs}=p_g+p_{atm}$$
Open surface/jet: gauge pressure about 0.
Unknown pipe pressure: NOT automatically zero.
Absolute pressure for vapor comparisons and gas density.
$$\rho=\frac{p_{abs}}{R_{gas}T_{abs}}$$
Air: Rgas about 287 J/(kg K).
Temperature in Kelvin.
