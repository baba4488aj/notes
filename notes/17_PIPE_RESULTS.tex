Hagen Poiseuille

HORIZONTAL FULLY DEVELOPED LAMINAR ROUND PIPE.
Radius R; diameter D=2R; dp/dz < 0 for +z flow.

VELOCITY PROFILE
$$u(r)=-\frac{1}{4\mu}\frac{dp}{dz}(R^2-r^2)$$

MAX AND MEAN
$$u_{max}=-\frac{R^2}{4\mu}\frac{dp}{dz}$$
$$\bar V=-\frac{R^2}{8\mu}\frac{dp}{dz}$$
$$\bar V=\frac{1}{2}u_{max}$$

VOLUME FLOW
$$Q=\pi R^2\bar V$$
$$Q=-\frac{\pi R^4}{8\mu}\frac{dp}{dz}$$

PRESSURE GRADIENT
$$\frac{dp}{dz}=-\frac{8\mu Q}{\pi R^4}$$
$$\frac{dp}{dz}=-\frac{32\mu\bar V}{D^2}$$

PRESSURE DROP OVER L
$$\Delta p=\frac{32\mu L\bar V}{D^2}$$
$$\Delta p=\frac{128\mu LQ}{\pi D^4}$$

PIPE WITH AXIAL GRAVITY
Define $G=-dp/dz+\rho g_z$.
$$u(r)=\frac{G}{4\mu}(R^2-r^2)$$
$$\bar V=\frac{GR^2}{8\mu}$$
For inclined/vertical flow, pressure drop and frictional loss differ because elevation changes.

Verify Re is laminar AFTER calculating Q/V.
