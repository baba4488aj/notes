Flow Area Integrals

GENERAL FORMULAS
$$Q=\int_Au\,dA$$
$$\bar V=\frac{Q}{A}$$
$$\dot m=\rho Q$$

PLATES: width b, full gap h.
$$dA=b\,dy$$
$$Q=b\int_0^h u(y)\,dy$$
$$\bar V=\frac{1}{h}\int_0^h u(y)\,dy$$

ROUND PIPE: ring area dA = 2*pi*r*dr.
$$Q=2\pi\int_0^R u(r)r\,dr$$
$$\bar V=\frac{2}{R^2}\int_0^R u(r)r\,dr$$

WHY THE FACTOR r?
Outer rings represent more cross-sectional area than inner rings.

STATIONARY PLATE PARABOLA
$$u=u_{max}(1-\frac{4\eta^2}{h^2})$$
Integrating from -h/2 to h/2:
$$\bar V=\frac{2}{3}u_{max}$$

LAMINAR PIPE PARABOLA
$$u=u_{max}(1-\frac{r^2}{R^2})$$
Integrating with r dr:
$$\bar V=\frac{1}{2}u_{max}$$

POWER-LAW PROFILE FAMILY
If $u=u_{max}[1-(r/R)^n]$:
$$\frac{\bar V}{u_{max}}=\frac{n}{n+2}$$
Only valid for that exact family.

MOVING PLATES: never assume either stationary plate or pipe mean/max ratio.
