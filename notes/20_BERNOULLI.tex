Bernoulli Setup

STEADY, INCOMPRESSIBLE, INVISCID, with gravity, along the same streamline:

PER MASS (J/kg)
$$\frac{p}{\rho}+\frac{V^2}{2}+gz=C$$
PER VOLUME (Pa)
$$p+\frac{\rho V^2}{2}+\rho gz=C$$
PER WEIGHT / HEAD (m)
$$\frac{p}{\rho g}+\frac{V^2}{2g}+z=C$$

WHEN TO USE EXTENDED ENERGY
Pumps, turbines, viscous losses, fittings or strong dissipation: use the total mechanical-energy equation rather than ideal Bernoulli.

FIVE-STEP METHOD
1. Choose point where p or V is known (free surface, jet, stagnation, measured station).
2. Label p, V, z for BOTH stations.
3. Establish gauge or absolute p reference.
4. Use continuity for speeds and write FULL Bernoulli before cancellations.
5. Rearrange symbolically; check sign and dimensions.

CONTINUITY FOR TWO ROUND SECTIONS
$$A_1\bar V_1=A_2\bar V_2$$
$$\frac{\bar V_2}{\bar V_1}=(\frac{D_1}{D_2})^2$$

An atmospheric boundary has gauge p=0; an unknown pipe station does not.
A large reservoir surface may have V about 0, not necessarily a pipe exit.
