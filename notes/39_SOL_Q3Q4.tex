Solutions Q3 and Q4

Q3 STREAM FUNCTION
Given u=ax+by, v=-ay; a=2, b=3.
Divergence ux+vy=a-a=0.
Integrate psi_y=u:
$$\psi=axy+\frac{b}{2}y^2+F(x)$$
Match -psi_x=v -> F'=0.
$$\psi=2xy+1.5y^2+C$$

Q3 ACCELERATION
At any x,y, after expanding convective terms:
$$a_x=a^2x,\quad a_y=a^2y$$
At (1,2): a=(4,8) m/s^2.
With rho=1000, horizontal plane:
px=-4000 Pa/m; py=-8000 Pa/m.

Q3 ROTATION
$$\zeta_z=v_x-u_y=-b=-3$$
$$\Omega_z=-1.5\ \text{1/s}$$
Rotational (clockwise local spin).

Q4 LAMINAR PIPE
Given D=.0200, L=2, mu=.10, rho=1000, umax=.200.
$$\bar V=\frac{1}{2}u_{max}=0.100$$
$$Q=\frac{\pi D^2}{4}\bar V$$
=3.14159e-5 m^3/s.
$$Re=\frac{\rho\bar VD}{\mu}=20$$
Laminar.
$$\Delta p=\frac{32\mu L\bar V}{D^2}$$
=1600 Pa.
$$|\tau_w|=\frac{8\mu\bar V}{D}$$
=4.00 Pa.
$$\nu=\frac{\mu}{\rho}=0.0001$$
$$Q_{max}=\frac{\pi D\nu Re_{crit}}{4}$$
=0.00361283 m^3/s for Recrit=2300. Threshold estimate, not guaranteed transition point.
