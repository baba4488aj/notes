Navier Stokes to ODE

Constant rho, mu Newtonian equation:
$$\rho\vec a=-\nabla p+\rho\vec g+\mu\nabla^2\vec V$$
With $\vec a=D\vec V/Dt$.

X COMPONENT
$$\rho(u_t+uu_x+vu_y+wu_z)$$
$$=-p_x+\rho g_x$$
$$+\mu(u_{xx}+u_{yy}+u_{zz})$$

ASSUME steady, fully developed, parallel wide-plate flow $u=u(y)$, v=w=0. Then time/convective terms, uxx, uzz vanish.

REDUCED x MOMENTUM
$$0=-\frac{dp}{dx}+\rho g_x+\mu u''$$
Define driving gradient:
$$G=-\frac{dp}{dx}+\rho g_x$$
Then:
$$\mu u''=-G$$
Integrate ONCE:
$$u'=-\frac{G}{\mu}y+C_1$$
Integrate TWICE:
$$u=-\frac{G}{2\mu}y^2+C_1y+C_2$$

Use TWO independent boundary conditions to find C1,C2. This same quadratic solves Couette, Poiseuille, and belt-film cases; only BCs change.

Check by differentiating twice, substituting each boundary, testing G=0 and dimensions.

SIGN RULE
With +x inlet to outlet, positive pressure drop $\Delta p=p_{in}-p_{out}$:
$$\frac{dp}{dx}=-\frac{\Delta p}{L}$$
