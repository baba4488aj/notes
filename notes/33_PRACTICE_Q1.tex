QUESTION 1 (20 POINTS)
MOVING PLATE + OPPOSING PRESSURE

Newtonian liquid mu=0.20 Pa s, rho=900 kg/m^3 between wide horizontal plates:
y=0 fixed, y=h=0.004m upper plate moving +x at U=0.60m/s.

Given pressure gradient:
$$\frac{dp}{dx}=+30000\ \text{Pa/m}$$
Width b=0.50m. Steady incompressible fully developed laminar flow, negligible sidewalls; gx=0.

(a) 4pt: State both boundary conditions; reduce x momentum equation.

(b) 6pt: Derive u(y), calculate alphaC, signed Q.

(c) 6pt: Find any interior u=0 and interior extremum. Classify it; give velocity there. Compare wall values for GLOBAL extrema.

(d) 4pt: Calculate SIGNED $\tau_{xy}$ at each wall.

TIPS
Pressure rises in +x, so it opposes upper-wall motion. Do not assume max at gap midpoint or use fixed-plate mean/max ratio.
