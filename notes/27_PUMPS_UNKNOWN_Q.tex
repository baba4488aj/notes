UNKNOWN Q
1. Convert every segment velocity to Q/A.
2. Write full extended energy equation.
3. If f and K known, solve algebraically for Q.
4. If f depends on Re, iterate.

OPEN RESERVOIRS, NO PUMP
With outlet loss INCLUDED:
$$\Delta z=(f_D\frac{L}{D}+\sum K)\frac{\bar V^2}{2g}$$
Both endpoints are reservoir SURFACES, speeds about 0.

IF ENDPOINT IS A FREE JET
No exit-to-reservoir K:
$$\Delta z=(\alpha_{E,exit}+f_D\frac{L}{D}$$
$$+\sum K_{other})\frac{\bar V^2}{2g}$$
Do not double count exit kinetic energy.

TURBULENT, f UNKNOWN
Guess f -> solve Q -> calculate Re -> update Moody/Colebrook f -> repeat to consistency.

LAMINAR ROUND PIPE
Substitute f=64/Re; major loss proportional to Q, not Q^2.
Always recheck flow regime.

CHANGING DIAMETER
At constant Q and f:
$$H_{major}\propto\frac{LQ^2}{D^5}$$
For laminar round pipe:
$$\Delta p\propto\frac{\mu LQ}{D^4}$$
Real f changes with Re and roughness ratio. These are conditional scaling relations.
