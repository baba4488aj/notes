Couette Backflow

SPECIAL CASE: lower wall fixed, upper moves at U>0, no axial gravity.
$$Y=\frac{y}{h}$$
$$\alpha_C=-\frac{h^2}{2\mu U}\frac{dp}{dx}$$

NONDIMENSIONAL VELOCITY
$$\frac{u}{U}=Y+\alpha_CY(1-Y)$$
Mean:
$$\frac{\bar V}{U}=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{\alpha_C}{6}$$

INTERPRETATION
alpha_C > 0: pressure assists the wall.
alpha_C < 0: pressure opposes wall.

ZERO NET FLOW
$$\alpha_C=-3$$
Zero Q does NOT mean u=0 everywhere.

BACKFLOW NEAR FIXED WALL
$$\alpha_C<-1$$
Interior zero if physical:
$$Y_0=1+\frac{1}{\alpha_C}$$

STATIONARY POINT
$$Y_*=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{2\alpha_C}$$
Keep only if 0 <= Y* <= 1.
For alpha_C > 1: interior max.
For alpha_C < -1: interior min.
Always compare both wall velocities.

Pure Couette when G=0:
$$u=\frac{Uy}{h}$$
$$\bar V=\frac{U}{2}$$
$$\tau=\frac{\mu U}{h}$$
Moving wall alone does not establish dp/dx=0.
