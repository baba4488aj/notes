Belt Zero Net Flow

VERTICAL UPWARD-MOVING BELT
Take x positive DOWN, Uw=-Vb.
G = rho*g; stress-free surface.
$$\bar V=-V_b+\frac{\rho gh^2}{3\mu}$$

ZERO FLOW CONDITION
$$Q=0\Rightarrow\bar V=0$$
$$V_b=\frac{\rho gh^2}{3\mu}$$

LET $Y=y/h$:
$$\frac{u}{\rho gh^2/\mu}$$
$$=Y-\frac{Y^2}{2}-\frac{1}{3}$$

REVERSAL POINT
Solve u=0:
$$Y^2-2Y+\frac{2}{3}=0$$
$$Y=1-\frac{1}{\sqrt3}$$
Physical root Y about 0.423.
The other root exceeds the film thickness.

AT BOUNDARIES
At belt:
$$u(0)=-\frac{\rho gh^2}{3\mu}$$
At free surface:
$$u(h)=\frac{\rho gh^2}{6\mu}$$

Why Q=0 although flow moves? Signed positive and negative areas under u(y) cancel.

SKETCH STEPS
Mark wall and surface speeds, curvature u''=-G/mu, zero crossing and du/dy=0 at free surface. Plot-axis orientation matters.
