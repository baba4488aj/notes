QUESTION 2 SOLUTION
Q=.0100 m^3/s; D=.100m; L=30m; f=.025; rho=1000; eta=.75.

(a) Flow area, mean speed:
$$A=\frac{\pi D^2}{4}=0.007854$$
$$\bar V=\frac{Q}{A}=1.27324$$
$$\frac{\bar V^2}{2g}=0.0826269\ \text{m}$$

Major:
$$H_{major}=f\frac{L}{D}\frac{\bar V^2}{2g}$$
= 0.61970m.
Fittings:
$$\sum K=0.50+3(0.30)+1=2.40$$
Minor = 2.4(0.0826269) = .19830m.
Total HL=0.81801m.

(b) Open reservoir surface energy:
$$H_p=12.0+0.81801=12.8180$$
$$P_{hyd}=\rho gQH_p$$
=1257.45 W.
$$P_{in}=\frac{P_{hyd}}{0.75}$$
=1676.60W, about 1.68 kW.

(c) Suction: Ls=4m; entrance K=.50, ONE elbow K=.30, pump inlet 1m above source.
$$H_{L,s}=[f\frac{L_s}{D}+0.8]\frac{\bar V^2}{2g}$$
=0.148728m.
Source surface to pump inlet:
$$0=\frac{p_{s,g}}{\rho g}+\frac{\bar V^2}{2g}$$
$$+1.00+H_{L,s}$$
Thus:
$$p_{s,g}=-\rho g(1+0.0826269$$
$$+0.148728)$$
= -12079.6Pa = -12.08kPa gauge.
This is negative gauge, not negative absolute.
