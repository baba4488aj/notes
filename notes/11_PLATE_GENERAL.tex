Two Moving Plates

FULL GAP h, wall positions y=0 and y=h. Signed wall speeds U0 and U1. Pressure/gravity forcing:
$$G=-\frac{dp}{dx}+\rho g_x$$

BCs:
$u(0)=U_0$, $u(h)=U_1$.

GENERAL PROFILE
$$u=U_0+\frac{U_1-U_0}{h}y$$
$$+\frac{G}{2\mu}y(h-y)$$

AVERAGE AND FLOW
$$\bar V=\frac{U_0+U_1}{2}+\frac{Gh^2}{12\mu}$$
$$q'=h\bar V$$
$$Q=bh\bar V$$

SHEAR PROFILE
$$\tau_{xy}=\mu\frac{U_1-U_0}{h}$$
$$+\frac{G}{2}(h-2y)$$

DERIVATION FROM ODE
$u=-Gy^2/(2\mu)+C_1y+C_2$.
BC y=0: C2 = U0.
BC y=h:
$$C_1=\frac{U_1-U_0}{h}+\frac{Gh}{2\mu}$$

TRICKS
- Opposite plate motion? One U must be negative.
- No pressure forcing? G=0 if gx=0 too.
- Given Q? Solve average formula for G.
- Given wall stress? Differentiate profile and evaluate wall.
- Given zero net transport? Set Q=0; u need not vanish.
- For speed extrema solve u'=0 AND compare two walls.
