Nozzle and Siphon

NOZZLE TO ATMOSPHERE, IDEAL
Outlet gauge pressure 0.
$$p_{1,g}=\rho[\frac{V_2^2-V_1^2}{2}$$
$$+g(z_2-z_1)]$$
Compute V1 from area continuity first.
Bends change direction, but scalar Bernoulli uses speed squared.

LARGE OPEN TANK -> FREE JET
$$V_{out}=\sqrt{2g\Delta z}$$
If tank surface area At is finite and exit Ao:
$$V_{surface}=\frac{A_o}{A_t}V_{out}$$
$$V_{out}=\sqrt{\frac{2g\Delta z}{1-(A_o/A_t)^2}}$$
Quasi-steady ideal model.

SIPHON CREST PRESSURE
From source free surface to crest c:
$$p_{c,abs}=p_{atm}+\rho g(z_s-z_c)$$
$$-\frac{\rho V_c^2}{2}-\rho gH_{L,s\to c}$$

VAPOR-PRESSURE LIMIT
$$p_{c,abs}\ge p_{vapor}$$
Maximum crest height above source:
$$z_c-z_s\le\frac{p_{atm}-p_{vapor}}{\rho g}$$
$$-\frac{V_c^2}{2g}-H_{L,s\to c}$$
Use absolute vapor pressure; include only losses UPSTREAM of crest.

STAGNATION / PITOT
$$q=\frac{\rho V^2}{2}$$
$$p_0=p+q$$
$$V=\sqrt{\frac{2(p_0-p)}{\rho}}$$
Differential U-tube at same elevation:
$$\Delta p=(\rho_m-\rho)g\Delta h$$
