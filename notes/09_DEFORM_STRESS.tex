Deformation and Stress

NORMAL STRAIN RATES
$$e_{xx}=u_x$$
$$e_{yy}=v_y$$
$$e_{zz}=w_z$$
Their sum is divergence.

ENGINEERING SHEAR RATE
$$\dot\gamma_{xy}=u_y+v_x$$
Tensor shear strain rate is HALF:
$$e_{xy}=\frac{1}{2}(u_y+v_x)$$

NEWTONIAN SHEAR STRESS
$$\tau_{xy}=\mu(u_y+v_x)$$
$$\tau_{yz}=\mu(v_z+w_y)$$
$$\tau_{zx}=\mu(w_x+u_z)$$

NORMAL STRESS (INCOMPRESSIBLE)
$$\sigma_{xx}=-p+2\mu u_x$$
Similarly for yy and zz.
General compressible Stokes model:
$$\sigma_{xx}=-p+2\mu u_x$$
$$-\frac{2}{3}\mu(\nabla\cdot\vec V)$$

Stokes hypothesis: zero bulk-viscosity combination; NOT mu=0.

SIMPLE AXIAL FLOW u(y)
$$\tau_{xy}=\mu\frac{du}{dy}$$
At wall, evaluate derivative at the correct y.
Signed stress and force direction can differ with surface normal and wall/fluid viewpoint.

WALL FORCE MAGNITUDE
$$F=|\tau_w|A_{wet}$$
$$P=F|U_{wall}|$$
Use wetted wall area, NOT flow cross-section.
