Moving Belt Film

COORDINATES
x positive DOWNHILL; y away from belt.
Belt at y=0; free surface y=h.
Signed belt speed Uw; uphill speed Vb means Uw=-Vb.
Inclined belt: $g_x=g\sin\theta$.
Vertical belt: gx=g.

Define:
$$G=-\frac{dp}{dx}+\rho g_x$$
Uniform atmospheric surface, no axial pressure gradient: dp/dx=0.

REDUCED EQUATION
$$\mu u''=-G$$
BC 1: $u(0)=U_w$ (no slip).
BC 2: $u'(h)=0$ (stress free, NOT u(h)=0).

PROFILE
$$u=U_w+\frac{G}{\mu}(hy-\frac{y^2}{2})$$

DERIVATION
$u'=-Gy/\mu+C_1$.
BC at y=h -> $C_1=Gh/\mu$.
Integrate u and set C2=Uw.

SHEAR, MEAN, FLOW
$$\tau_{xy}=G(h-y)$$
$$\bar V=U_w+\frac{Gh^2}{3\mu}$$
$$Q=bh\bar V$$
Surface speed:
$$u(h)=U_w+\frac{Gh^2}{2\mu}$$
Wall shear magnitude = |Gh|.

IMPOSED SURFACE SHEAR
If $\mu u'(h)=\tau_s$:
$$u=U_w+\frac{G}{\mu}(hy-\frac{y^2}{2})$$
$$+\frac{\tau_s}{\mu}y$$
$$\bar V=U_w+\frac{Gh^2}{3\mu}$$
$$+\frac{\tau_sh}{2\mu}$$
