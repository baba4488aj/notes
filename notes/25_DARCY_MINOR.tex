Darcy and Minor Loss

MAJOR PIPE HEAD LOSS
$$H_{major}=f_D\frac{L}{D}\frac{\bar V^2}{2g}$$
Darcy fD (NOT Fanning Cf).
Laminar round pipe:
$$f_D=\frac{64}{Re_D}$$
Turbulent: Moody chart using Re and relative roughness epsilon/D.

COLEBROOK (optional numerical check)
$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{f_D}}=-2\log[\frac{\epsilon}{3.7D}$$
$$+\frac{2.51}{Re_D\sqrt{f_D}}]$$
Use base-10 log. Iteration required. Chart may be supplied instead.

FITTINGS / MINOR LOSSES
$$H_{minor}=\sum_iK_i\frac{V_{ref,i}^2}{2g}$$
$$H_L=\sum H_{major}+\sum H_{minor}$$
For one common diameter:
$$H_L=(f_D\frac{L}{D}+\sum K)\frac{\bar V^2}{2g}$$

Different diameters: calculate EACH segment with its own V and Re.

SUDDEN EXPANSION
$$H_{exp}=\frac{(V_1-V_2)^2}{2g}$$
$$K_{exp}=(1-\frac{A_1}{A_2})^2$$
This K uses upstream smaller-pipe speed in the usual model.

DISCHARGE INTO LARGE RESERVOIR: exit K about 1.
FREE JET endpoint: keep exit kinetic energy; do NOT add reservoir-exit loss.

CONTRACTION K convention in lecture uses downstream small-pipe speed.

Measured major loss, level equal-D taps:
$$f_D=\frac{2D\Delta p}{\rho L\bar V^2}$$
Exclude fitting losses when inferring straight-pipe f.
