Solutions to Drills

A. ZERO FLOW FILM
$$\frac{y}{h}=1-\frac{1}{\sqrt3}=0.42265$$
Signed flow areas cancel. Speeds need not vanish.

B. UNSTEADY TUBE
$$\frac{dV}{dt}=\frac{6400}{800(4)}$$
=2.00 m/s^2.

C. IDEAL NOZZLE
$$V_1=8(\frac{20}{40})^2=2$$
Speed m/s.
$$p_{1,g}=\rho[\frac{8^2-2^2}{2}+g(3)]$$
=59,430 Pa =59.43kPa gauge.

D. GRADE LINES
Ideal same-D rising pipe: EGL constant, HGL constant; static pressure decreases by rho*g*2m.
With friction, EGL and HGL fall by HL, pressure decreases by rho*g*(2+HL).

E. STREAM FUNCTION
psi=2xy+y^2.
$$u=\psi_y=2x+2y$$
$$v=-\psi_x=-2y$$
Divergence 2-2=0.
Vorticity z: 0-2=-2 1/s.
$$q'=\psi(1,2)-\psi(1,0)=8$$
Units q' m^2/s per unit width.

F. IDEAL DRAIN TIME
$$t=\frac{2(1)}{0.001\sqrt{2(9.81)}}$$
$$\times(\sqrt1-\sqrt{0.25})$$
=225.76s =3.76min.
