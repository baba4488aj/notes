Assumptions and BCs

STEADY: time derivatives vanish; spatial acceleration may remain.

INCOMPRESSIBLE: constant density in these models, $\nabla\cdot\vec V=0$; NOT constant velocity.

FULLY DEVELOPED: axial profile does not change downstream; dp/dx and shear may be nonzero.

PARALLEL FLOW: transverse components vanish; for steady u(y), convective acceleration is zero.

INVISCID: negligible modeled viscosity. IRROTATIONAL: curl = 0. Different concepts.

WIDE PLATES: neglect edge effects.
PIPE: axial u(r), no swirl/radial flow when axisymmetric, fully developed.

BOUNDARIES
Fixed no-slip wall: fluid tangential velocity = 0.
Moving no-slip wall: fluid speed = SIGNED wall speed.
Symmetry plane or pipe center:
$$\frac{du}{dn}=0$$
Free surface with negligible gas shear: du/dn = 0, not u = 0.
Imposed surface shear: $\mu du/dn=\tau_s$ with consistent normal.
Fluid interface: tangential speed and shear stress continuous (usual model).

SETUP WARNING
A moving plate can destroy midpoint symmetry. Do not force du/dy = 0 at gap midpoint unless symmetry holds.
