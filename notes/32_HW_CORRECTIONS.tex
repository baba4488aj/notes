HW1 GRAVITY
If x,y both horizontal: gy=0 -> py=-rho*ay.
If y vertical upward: gy=-g -> py=rho*(-g-ay).
The PDF problem and shown solution use different orientations; explicitly specify gravity vector in your work.

HW13(a) CURL SIGN
Given:
$u=2x^2+y^2-x^2y$
$v=x^3+xy^2-2xy$
Then:
$$u_y=2y-x^2$$
$$v_x=3x^2+y^2-2y$$
$$\Omega_z=\frac{4x^2+y^2-4y}{2}$$
Do NOT forget to subtract u_y.
